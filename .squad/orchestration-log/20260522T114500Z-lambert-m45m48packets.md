## Key Learnings Recorded

- `client/packets.ex` and `server/packets.ex` are flat files — every packet is its own top-level `defmodule`. Append after the final `end` of the last module.
- `replace_string_in_file` on these files requires including the entire last `defmodule` line for uniqueness — repeated short closings cause multi-match failures.
- BOM-free UTF-8: use `[System.IO.File]::AppendAllText(path, text, New-Object System.Text.UTF8Encoding($false))`.
